Introduction

A Cybersecurity Analyst is responsible for identifying, analyzing, and responding to security incidents. They monitor systems, networks, and applications for vulnerabilities and threats.

Responsibilities

  • Monitor systems and networks for security incidents
  • Analyze logs and reports to identify potential threats
  • Respond to security incidents and breaches
  • Conduct vulnerability assessments and penetration testing
  • Develop and implement security policies and procedures

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field
  • Experience with cybersecurity tools and technologies (e.g., Splunk, SIEM)
  • Knowledge of cybersecurity principles and best practices
  • Strong analytical skills
